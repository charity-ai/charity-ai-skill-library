## Measurement

- [ ] UTM/campaign fields use a consistent naming convention.
- [ ] Contact source, campaign, diagnostic status, paid date, booking date, and opportunity value can be reported.
- [ ] A qualified call, booked appointment, opportunity, and closed revenue use distinct pipeline events.
- [ ] Buyer consent and messaging rules are documented.
- [ ] Baselines are captured before SEO, content, or link work begins.

## Go/no-go statement

**Go live only when:**

1. The buyer can submit a request, pay, book, receive confirmation, and receive a real meeting link.
2. The owner receives an internal alert and sees the correct contact/opportunity record.
3. Payment webhooks reject invalid signatures and support the active environment IDs.
4. The site displays only the business brand, buyer-facing terms, and approved proof.
