# 15-Minute Paid Revenue Diagnostic Call Script

Use this call to surface the revenue constraint, establish commercial seriousness, and determine whether the prospect qualifies for a paid implementation recommendation. Do not attempt to solve every issue on the call. The goal is a precise problem statement and a credible next decision.

## Before the call

Review the qualification form, website, service territory, Google Business presence, and any CRM or search data provided. Enter the call with one observable opportunity or question, not an unverified claim.

## 0:00–1:30 | Frame the conversation

> “Thank you for completing the diagnostic. This is a focused 15-minute working session. I want to identify the specific point where revenue is leaking, estimate what correcting it is worth, and determine whether an Authority Engine build is the right next move. I will ask direct questions, then I will tell you what I see and recommend a next step.”

Ask: “Does that agenda work for you?”

## 1:30–5:00 | Establish commercial context

Ask these questions in sequence. Use follow-up questions only where the answer exposes a meaningful constraint.

1. “Which one or two services create the best gross profit or highest-value jobs for you right now?”
2. “What is a typical job value for that service, and what does a great month look like in booked jobs?”
3. “Where are most new opportunities coming from today: organic search, referrals, ads, repeat business, or something else?”
4. “When someone calls after hours or submits a web request, what actually happens in the first five minutes?”
5. “What is the most expensive type of inquiry you know you are not consistently converting?”

Transition:

> “That gives me the economics. Now I want to understand whether the constraint is discovery, trust, or response speed.”

## 5:00–9:00 | Locate the dominant revenue leak

6. “When your ideal buyer searches for your highest-value service in your strongest city, what would you want them to find and believe before they call?”
7. “What evidence do you have on your current site that proves you are the lower-risk choice: certifications, local jobs, before-and-after evidence, documented outcomes, warranties, or specific expertise?”
8. “Which buyer questions or objections do your team answer repeatedly that are not answered well online?”
9. “Can you see which campaigns, pages, calls, or forms are creating real opportunities in your CRM today?”
10. “Where does the handoff break most often: missed call, slow reply, weak qualification, no appointment, no estimate follow-up, or a lack of management visibility?”

Optional calculation prompt:

> “If one additional [high-value service] job per month is worth approximately [job value], what would it mean financially to make that revenue path predictable?”

## 9:00–12:30 | Test implementation readiness

11. “Who owns the decision to change your lead capture, follow-up, or visibility system?”
12. “Do you have the capacity to take and close more of the right work if we improve volume or speed-to-lead?”
13. “Can you provide the proof assets and subject-matter input required to make the content and follow-up accurate?”
14. “What has stopped you from solving this before: internal bandwidth, poor vendors, unclear priorities, or a lack of a repeatable system?”
15. “If we identify a clear 90-day solution, what decision process would you need to follow to move forward?”

## 12:30–15:00 | Summarize and advance

Use this summary structure:

> “Based on what you shared, the highest-value issue is **[specific leak]**. The commercial consequence is **[missed opportunity / slow response / insufficient proof]** around **[service line]**. I would not recommend random content or another generic marketing campaign. I would recommend a focused authority and conversion system that solves **[specific gap]** first.”

If qualified:

> “The next move is for me to complete the written Revenue Map, the three commercial-search opportunities, the conversion audit, and a 90-day Authority Architecture. I will deliver it by **[date]**, then we will meet to decide whether you implement it internally or have Charity Loves AI install and operate it. Your $1,500 diagnostic fee is credited toward the launch fee if we move into implementation.”

If not qualified:

> “I do not think a full Authority Engine is the right investment until **[constraint]** is resolved. The most responsible next move is **[smaller corrective action]**. Once that is in place, we can reassess whether the diagnostic system has a viable return.”

## Notes to record immediately

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Priority service and city | Exact service line and geography |
| Job economics | Average job value and credible upside |
| Dominant leak | Discovery, proof, intake, response, appointment, or follow-up |
| Current stack | Website, CRM, call answering, forms, booking, analytics |
| Proof assets | Reviews, certifications, photos, results, case studies, sales recordings |
| Decision owner | Name, role, and decision process |
| Capacity | Sales and fulfillment ability to take more qualified work |
| Recommendation | Diagnostic output and implementation path |
| Next commitment | Delivery date, strategy call, or decline reason |
