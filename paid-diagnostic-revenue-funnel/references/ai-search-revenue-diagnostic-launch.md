# AI Search Revenue Diagnostic Launch Reference

## Commercial architecture

Use this sequence for a premium local-service AI-search offer:

1. **Targeted attention:** Reach a narrow ICP through personalized evidence, founder authority, partners, or high-intent search.
2. **Qualification request:** Capture a request using a native CRM form. A request is not payment or a booked appointment.
3. **Paid diagnostic:** Collect a one-time diagnostic fee before providing account-specific strategic work. State whether and when the fee is credited toward implementation.
4. **Paid-buyer booking:** Show the dedicated calendar only after payment confirmation. Create the Zoom meeting and purchaser confirmation from the booking system.
5. **Decision artifact:** Deliver a defined strategic output, then recommend a scoped launch and recurring implementation only where fit is present.
6. **Implementation retainer:** Operate the visibility, authority, conversion, CRM, and measurement system with a clear monthly cadence.

## AI Search Revenue Relevance System

| Layer | Required question | Typical work | Buyer-facing evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue truth | Which service, market, job type, and buyer matter most? | Margin, capacity, service-area, buyer, and proof review | Priority map |
| AI/local relevance | Is the brand eligible to be retrieved, cited, and trusted? | Prompt mapping, competitor review, local proof, entity, review, and technical audit | Baseline and gap map |
| Authority assets | What evidence answers high-value buyer questions? | Service, comparison, emergency, process, proof, and video assets | Publish-ready asset plan |
| Demand capture | Can a serious buyer take action without friction? | Calls, forms, calendar, payment, and lead handoff | Tested contact creation |
| Revenue conversion | Does the inquiry receive a fast accountable response? | CRM routing, follow-up, pipeline, owner assignment, and reminders | Appointment/opportunity audit |
| Commercial reporting | Does visibility translate into movement? | Qualified calls, bookings, opportunities, estimate rate, source, and closed revenue review | Monthly executive scorecard |

## $1,500 diagnostic default deliverable

Use a clear scope. The default output should include:

- A priority service and service-area revenue map.
- A priority AI-search and local-intent prompt set.
- A competitor, citation, proof, and conversion-gap assessment.
- A lead-capture and CRM handoff assessment.
- A 90-day implementation sequence with dependencies, metrics, and client responsibilities.
- A recommendation for a launch project and recurring implementation only if the economics and capacity support it.

Do not promise rankings, citations, appointments, or revenue. Explain attribution limits and operational dependencies such as review quality, response time, sales capacity, local competition, seasonality, and client approvals.

## Demand-generation priority

For a new premium diagnostic offer, prioritize channels in this order:

| Priority | Channel | Standard |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Named-account personalized outreach | 10–15 relevant 60–90 second video observations per weekday. Offer the paid diagnostic, not unpaid consulting. |
| 2 | Ecosystem partners | Work with credible non-competing partners who already serve the same ICP. Offer a useful benchmark, briefing, or member resource before asking for referrals. |
| 3 | Founder-led LinkedIn | Publish three operator-relevant posts weekly and engage with buyer/partner discussions daily. Use one diagnostic CTA. |
| 4 | Google Search Ads | Test only explicit commercial intent with controlled budget, strict negative keywords, and paid-diagnostic conversion tracking. |
| 5 | YouTube | Publish one useful long-form operator video each week and repurpose it into vertical clips. |
| 6 | Retargeting | Use only after qualified site/video audience volume exists. |

Do not lead with cold Meta advertising, broad social posting, or large-scale backlink work before the message has produced diagnostic conversations.

## Search Atlas operating order

1. Establish a project baseline before changes.
2. Map 20–30 commercial queries and 10 AI-search prompts.
3. Publish commercial and evidence-led assets before conducting link work.
4. Create one original local-industry benchmark, research asset, calculator, or data story.
5. Use cloud stacks only as a branded support layer for real assets, not as the core growth strategy.
6. Use competitor gaps for credible relationship, editorial, partner, and local-resource outreach.

Cloud stacks, automated links, and press-release volume cannot replace original evidence, third-party credibility, useful content, or direct prospect conversations. Avoid repetitive exact-match anchors, fake local entities, fabricated reviews, thin AI pages, and undisclosed community seeding.

## Core content-to-distribution pattern

Create one substantial asset, then repurpose it into a landing-page section, a LinkedIn post series, a YouTube video, 4–6 vertical clips, an email/newsletter issue, outreach evidence, and a partner briefing. Do not build a separate idea for every channel.
